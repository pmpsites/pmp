Blackpool, UK

Challenge:

Blackpool is famous as a visitor destination, but it’s a perception that obscures a much richer identity and a wider range of place benefits.

Solution:

We worked with the project partnership to reposition Blackpool as a location for business investment and career success in thriving high-technology sectors, supported by a compelling quality of life proposition.

Bicester Garden Town, UK

Challenge:

Bicester Garden Town is a new, sustainable community near Oxford, requiring an entirely new place proposition.

Solution:

Positioning Bicester within the Oxford region knowledge economy, we developed a place brand and data-supported proposition combining high-value employment opportunities with quantified quality of life advantages.

Challenge:

Central Bedfordshire is a key growth area at the heart of the Oxford-Cambridge region - but it requires recognition and a defined place proposition.

Solution:

We developed a place proposition for skilled workers and new residents combining knowledge economy career opportunities with quantified lifestyle benefits - presented through engaging imagery and informative location data.
